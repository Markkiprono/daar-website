Our story

A door on Ngong Road

Daar means home. We built one room around one idea — that the things worth eating can't be hurried. Grey plaster, steel, a red bench by the window, and a kitchen that never quite stops.

Everything is made here. Nothing arrives frozen. The pastry you pick up at seven was folded the night before by someone who will be there when you walk in.
